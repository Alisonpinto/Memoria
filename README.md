# About Memoria

Memoria is a modern social saving and discovery app where users can save products, links, images, ideas, or anything meaningful to their profile, and those saved items can automatically appear in a global community feed.

The goal of the project is to create a clean and modern social experience focused around:

* saving meaningful things
* discovering what others are saving
* interacting through shared interests
* building a modern community-driven feed

The UI and experience are heavily inspired by modern social platforms like Reddit, Discord, and Threads while maintaining its own unique identity.

---

# Project Vision

People save things because those things matter to them.

Memoria aims to transform saved content into a discoverable social experience where users can explore interesting products, ideas, links, and collections shared by people all around the world.

---

# Logo Meaning

The app mascot/logo concept is based on a **kangaroo**.

Why?

A kangaroo carries and protects valuable things inside its pouch.

That perfectly represents the core idea behind Memoria:

* saving things
* keeping valuable content safe
* carrying memories and discoveries
* personal storage with social discovery

The logo direction is minimalist, modern, startup-inspired, and designed for dark UI environments.
